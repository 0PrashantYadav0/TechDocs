# Authentication & Authorization

Authentication (AuthN) and Authorization (AuthZ) are the bedrock of any secure application. Getting them wrong doesn't just mean a bug; it means a data breach, a PR nightmare, and potentially the end of your company. This module will take you from the basics of identity to advanced distributed authorization patterns.

| # | Chapter | Level | What you'll learn |
|---|---------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | [Authentication Fundamentals](./authentication-fundamentals.md) | Basic | AuthN vs AuthZ, passwords, sessions vs tokens |
| 2 | [OAuth 2.0 and OIDC](./oauth2-and-oidc.md) | Intermediate | Delegation, grant types, identity layer |
| 3 | [JWT and Token Security](./jwt-and-token-security.md) | Intermediate | JWT structure, signing, storage, revocation |
| 4 | [Authorization Patterns](./authorization-patterns.md) | Advanced | RBAC, ABAC, ReBAC, centralized policies |
