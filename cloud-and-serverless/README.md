# Cloud & Serverless

Welcome to the Cloud & Serverless module. This module breaks down the modern cloud landscape, skipping the vendor marketing fluff and focusing on the architectural patterns, trade-offs, and practical realities of building systems in the cloud. We'll start with the foundational concepts of cloud-native development, move into the specifics of serverless architectures, and finish with advanced cloud architecture patterns for scale and resilience.

## Contents

| # | Chapter | Level | What you'll learn |
|---|---------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | [Cloud Native Fundamentals](./cloud-native-fundamentals.md) | Basic | 12-factor apps, service models (IaaS to FaaS), regions vs. AZs, and the shared responsibility model. |
| 2 | [Serverless Patterns](./serverless-patterns.md) | Intermediate | FaaS execution, cold starts, event-driven architectures, serverless DBs, and cost modeling. |
| 3 | [Cloud Architecture Patterns](./cloud-architecture-patterns.md) | Advanced | Multi-region setups, disaster recovery (RPO/RTO), cost optimization, and well-architected frameworks. |
