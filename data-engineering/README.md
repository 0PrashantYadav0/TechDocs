Data is the new oil, right? Sure, it's a nice cliché, but it misses the entire point. Raw oil is completely useless without refineries, pipelines, and logistics to turn it into fuel. The same goes for data. Without data engineering, all you have is a massive, expensive swamp of unstructured logs and outdated rows. Data engineering is the infrastructure that makes data actually valuable—building the pipelines, storage systems, and computation layers that turn raw noise into actionable signals.

Welcome to the data engineering module. Here we'll start with the fundamentals of moving data around and scale all the way up to processing endless streams of real-time events.

## Takeaways

- Data engineering is the foundation of data science, analytics, and AI.
- It's all about building robust refineries for your raw data.
- The field is constantly evolving from batch to streaming and from rigid warehouses to flexible lakehouses.
